The Pine Tree State

Our Maine Adventure

Maine offers us the rugged beauty of New England's coast combined with pristine wilderness areas, from the dramatic rocky shores of Acadia to the remote peaks of Baxter State Park. The Pine Tree State provides classic Northeast hiking through dense forests, past pristine lakes, and along one of America's most beautiful coastlines.

Our Maine hiking adventure will take us through ancient forests, up granite peaks, and along windswept coastal cliffs. We're excited to experience the wild beauty and maritime culture that make Maine a premier destination for outdoor enthusiasts.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🏔️ Mount Katahdin - Northern terminus of Appalachian Trail
  • 🌊 Acadia National Park - Rocky coast and mountain hiking
  • 🌲 100-Mile Wilderness - Remote Appalachian Trail section
  • 🦞 Maine Island Trail - Coastal kayaking and hiking
  • 💧 Gulf Hagas - "Grand Canyon of Maine"
