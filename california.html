Our California Adventure

California offers us perhaps the most diverse hiking experiences of any state - from the towering redwoods of the north to the desert landscapes of the south, from the granite peaks of the Sierra Nevada to the dramatic Pacific coastline. The Golden State promises adventures through some of America's most iconic national parks and breathtaking wilderness areas.

Our California hiking journey will take us through multiple climate zones and ecosystems in a single state. We're excited to experience everything from alpine lakes and snow-capped peaks to coastal redwood groves and desert canyons. California represents the pinnacle of hiking diversity and natural beauty.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🌲 Yosemite National Park - Granite cliffs, waterfalls, and giant sequoias
  • 🏔️ Sierra Nevada Range - High country lakes and mountain peaks
  • 🌊 Big Sur Coastline - Dramatic coastal cliffs and ocean views
  • 🏜️ Joshua Tree National Park - Desert landscapes and unique rock formations
  • 🌳 Redwood National Park - World's tallest trees and misty forests
