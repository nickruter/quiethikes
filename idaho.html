Our Idaho Adventure

Idaho offers us rugged mountain wilderness, pristine alpine lakes, and some of the most remote and unspoiled hiking in the American West. The Gem State provides challenging adventures through the Sawtooth Mountains, vast wilderness areas, and dramatic river canyons that few hikers ever experience.

Our Idaho hiking adventure will take us through towering granite peaks, across high alpine meadows, and along crystal-clear mountain streams. We're excited to discover the wild beauty and solitude that make Idaho a true gem for serious outdoor enthusiasts.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🏔️ Sawtooth National Recreation Area - Jagged peaks and alpine lakes
  • 🌲 Frank Church Wilderness - America's largest wilderness area
  • 🏞️ Craters of the Moon - Unique volcanic landscape
  • 💧 Shoshone Falls - "Niagara of the West"
  • ⛰️ Sun Valley - World-class mountain recreation
