Vermont

The Green Mountain State

Our Vermont Adventure

Vermont offers us classic New England hiking through the Green Mountains, pristine forests, and charming rural landscapes that epitomize autumn beauty. The Green Mountain State provides peaceful outdoor adventures through well-maintained trails and scenic countryside.

Our Vermont hiking adventure will take us through maple-covered mountains, past covered bridges, and along historic trails. We're excited to experience the quintessential New England beauty and seasonal splendor that make Vermont a hiking treasure.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🏔️ Mount Mansfield - Vermont's highest peak
  • 🍂 Long Trail - America's oldest long-distance hiking trail
  • 🌲 Green Mountain National Forest - Vermont's wilderness backbone
  • 🦌 Northeast Kingdom - Remote and pristine region
  • 🍁 Camel's Hump - Iconic Vermont summit with alpine zone
