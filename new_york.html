Our New York Adventure

New York State offers us incredible hiking diversity far beyond the famous city, from the High Peaks of the Adirondacks to the rolling hills of the Catskills, from Finger Lakes wine country to the dramatic gorges of central New York. The Empire State provides world-class hiking opportunities throughout diverse regions.

Our New York hiking adventure will take us through ancient mountains, past pristine lakes, and along historic trails. We're excited to explore the natural magnificence that makes New York a premier East Coast hiking destination.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🏔️ Adirondack High Peaks - 46 peaks over 4,000 feet
  • 🍂 Catskill Mountains - Historic hiking region with waterfalls
  • 🌊 Finger Lakes - Glacial lakes and gorge trails
  • 🦌 Thousand Islands - St. Lawrence River wilderness
  • 💧 Watkins Glen State Park - Rainbow Falls and gorge trail
