Our South Dakota Adventure

South Dakota exceeded all our expectations with its dramatic landscapes and rich cultural heritage. From the otherworldly formations of the Badlands to the sacred Black Hills, our hiking adventures in the Mount Rushmore State revealed stunning geological wonders and deeply meaningful historical sites. The vast prairies, rugged canyons, and granite spires created a diverse hiking experience unlike anywhere else in the country.

The state's Native American heritage added profound depth to our outdoor experiences. Walking through landscapes that have been sacred to indigenous peoples for thousands of years gave us a deeper appreciation for the land and its cultural significance. South Dakota's wide-open spaces and endless skies reminded us of the true scale and beauty of the American West.

Hiking Highlights:

  • 🏜️ Badlands National Park - Otherworldly rock formations and fossils
  • ⛰️ Black Hills - Sacred mountains and granite peaks
  • 🗿 Mount Rushmore area - Iconic monument and surrounding trails
  • 🌋 Devils Tower vicinity - Dramatic volcanic neck formations
  • 🦌 Custer State Park - Wildlife viewing and scenic drives
