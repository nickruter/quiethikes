Our Washington Adventure

Washington offers us some of the most spectacular and diverse hiking in America, from temperate rainforests to volcanic peaks, from rugged coastlines to high alpine meadows. The Evergreen State provides world-class outdoor adventures through incredibly varied Pacific Northwest ecosystems.

Our Washington hiking adventure will take us through ancient forests, up glacier-capped volcanoes, and along pristine coastlines. We're excited to explore the natural magnificence that makes Washington a premier destination for serious outdoor enthusiasts.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🏔️ Mount Rainier National Park - Iconic stratovolcano with glaciers
  • 🌲 Olympic National Park - Temperate rainforest and rugged coast
  • 🌋 North Cascades National Park - "American Alps"
  • 🌊 San Juan Islands - Island hopping and marine hiking
  • 💧 Snoqualmie Falls - Dramatic waterfall near Seattle
