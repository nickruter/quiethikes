Our Florida Adventure

Florida surprises many hikers with its diverse ecosystems beyond the famous beaches - from the mysterious Everglades to ancient cypress swamps, from coastal dunes to rolling hill country. The Sunshine State offers us unique subtropical hiking experiences found nowhere else in the continental United States.

Our Florida hiking adventure will take us through sawgrass prairies, along pristine beaches, and into mysterious swamplands teeming with wildlife. We're excited to explore the natural side of Florida that showcases incredible biodiversity and surprising terrain variations.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🌾 Everglades National Park - River of grass and unique wetland ecosystem
  • 🌊 Dry Tortugas National Park - Remote coral reef island paradise
  • 🌲 Big Cypress National Preserve - Ancient swamp forests and wildlife
  • 🏖️ Canaveral National Seashore - Pristine coastal hiking trails
  • 🦎 Fakahatchee Strand - World's largest stand of native royal palms
