Our New Hampshire Adventure

New Hampshire's White Mountains provided us with some of the most challenging and rewarding hikes of our journey. The granite peaks, pristine alpine lakes, and legendary mountain huts created an unforgettable hiking experience. From the rugged Presidential Range to the scenic Franconia Ridge, every trail in the Live Free or Die state tested our endurance while rewarding us with breathtaking views and a deep sense of accomplishment.

The state's well-established hiking culture and the Appalachian Mountain Club's hut system made our multi-day adventures both challenging and comfortable. New Hampshire's autumn foliage season provided a spectacular backdrop for our hikes, with vibrant colors painting the mountainsides in brilliant reds, oranges, and yellows.

Hiking Highlights:

  • ⛰️ Mount Washington - Northeast's highest peak with extreme weather
  • 🍂 Franconia Ridge - Spectacular fall foliage and ridge walking
  • 🏔️ Presidential Traverse - Epic multi-day mountain adventure
  • 💎 Cathedral Ledge - Dramatic cliff views and rock climbing
  • 🌊 Lake Winnipesaukee - Scenic lake hiking and island views
