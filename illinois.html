Our Illinois Adventure

Illinois may surprise many hikers with its diverse landscapes beyond the famous Chicago skyline - from rolling prairie grasslands to scenic river valleys, from ancient forests to peaceful lakeshores. The Prairie State offers us a chance to experience the natural beauty of America's heartland.

Our Illinois hiking adventure will take us through restored tallgrass prairies, along historic rivers, and past stunning geological formations. We're excited to explore the often-overlooked natural treasures that showcase Illinois's rich ecological heritage.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🌾 Midewin National Tallgrass Prairie - Restored prairie ecosystem
  • 🏞️ Starved Rock State Park - Dramatic canyons and waterfalls
  • 🌊 Illinois & Michigan Canal Trail - Historic waterway path
  • 🦅 Cahokia Mounds - Ancient Native American city
  • 🌳 Shawnee National Forest - Southern Illinois wilderness
