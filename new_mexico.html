Our New Mexico Adventure

New Mexico truly enchants hikers with its diverse landscapes from high desert mesas to snow-capped peaks, from ancient pueblo ruins to otherworldly rock formations. The Land of Enchantment offers us hiking adventures through landscapes that blend natural beauty with rich cultural heritage.

Our New Mexico hiking adventure will take us through colorful canyons, up sacred mountains, and past ancient archaeological sites. We're excited to explore the mystical beauty and cultural richness that make New Mexico a truly enchanting destination.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🏜️ White Sands National Park - Gypsum sand dune wilderness
  • 🏔️ Sangre de Cristo Mountains - Sacred peaks and alpine forests
  • 🌋 Bandelier National Monument - Ancient cliff dwellings
  • ⛰️ Carlsbad Caverns - Underground limestone chambers
  • 🎨 Ghost Ranch - O'Keeffe country red rock landscapes
