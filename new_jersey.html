Our New Jersey Adventure

New Jersey surprises many with its diverse natural beauty from pristine Atlantic beaches to rolling hills and ancient forests, all within easy reach of major cities. The Garden State offers us unexpected hiking gems including the Appalachian Trail, Pine Barrens wilderness, and scenic coastal areas.

Our New Jersey hiking adventure will take us through surprisingly wild areas, past historic sites, and along beautiful shorelines. We're excited to discover the natural treasures that make New Jersey much more than just urban landscapes.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🌲 Pine Barrens - Million-acre wilderness ecosystem
  • 🏔️ Appalachian Trail - New Jersey section through Kittatinny Mountains
  • 🌊 Island Beach State Park - Pristine barrier island
  • 🦅 Great Swamp National Wildlife Refuge - Important wetland habitat
  • 💧 Delaware Water Gap - Scenic river gorge
