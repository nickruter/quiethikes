Our Missouri Adventure

Missouri offers us diverse hiking through the Ozark Mountains, along scenic rivers, and past unique geological formations like natural springs and caves. The Show-Me State provides beautiful trail experiences through landscapes that showcase the heart of America's heartland.

Our Missouri hiking adventure will take us through rolling hills, dense forests, and along crystal-clear streams. We're excited to explore the natural wonders and outdoor recreation opportunities that make Missouri a hidden gem for hiking enthusiasts.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🏞️ Ozark National Scenic Riverways - America's first National Scenic Riverway
  • 🌊 Current River - Crystal-clear spring-fed waters
  • ⛰️ Taum Sauk Mountain - Missouri's highest peak
  • 🦌 Mark Twain National Forest - Ozark wilderness areas
  • 💧 Ha Ha Tonka State Park - Natural bridge and springs
