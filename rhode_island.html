Our Rhode Island Adventure

Rhode Island may be America's smallest state, but it offers us beautiful coastal hiking, historic trails, and charming New England landscapes packed into a compact area. The Ocean State provides intimate hiking experiences along pristine shorelines and through well-preserved natural areas.

Our Rhode Island hiking adventure will take us along dramatic coastlines, through peaceful forests, and past historic landmarks. We're excited to discover the coastal beauty and maritime heritage that make Rhode Island a delightful hiking gem.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🌊 Block Island - Pristine island with coastal hiking trails
  • 🏖️ Newport Cliff Walk - Scenic coastal trail past Gilded Age mansions
  • 🌲 Arcadia Management Area - Largest tract of state land
  • 🦆 Ninigret National Wildlife Refuge - Coastal pond ecosystem
  • ⛵ Narragansett Bay - Island hopping and coastal trails
