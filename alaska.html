Our Alaska Adventure

Alaska represents the ultimate hiking adventure - a land of glaciers, towering peaks, vast wilderness, and incredible wildlife. The Last Frontier promises to challenge us with some of the most remote and spectacular hiking experiences in North America. From the towering peaks of Denali to the coastal fjords of the Inside Passage, Alaska will test our limits while rewarding us with unparalleled natural beauty.

Our Alaska expedition will be the crown jewel of our 50-state hiking adventure. We're preparing for encounters with grizzly bears, caribou, and eagles, while hiking through landscapes that remain virtually unchanged since the last ice age. Alaska will be our ultimate test of endurance and our greatest reward.

Planned Epic Destinations:

  • 🏔️ Denali National Park - North America's tallest peak and pristine wilderness
  • 🐻 Katmai National Park - Brown bear viewing and volcanic landscapes
  • ❄️ Glacier Bay National Park - Tidewater glaciers and marine wildlife
  • 🌋 Wrangell-St. Elias - Largest national park with massive mountain ranges
  • 🦅 Kenai Fjords National Park - Coastal hiking and sea life viewing
