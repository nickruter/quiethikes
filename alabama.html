Our Alabama Adventure

Alabama awaits us with its diverse landscapes ranging from the Appalachian Mountains in the north to the Gulf Coast beaches in the south. We're excited to explore the Heart of Dixie's hiking opportunities, from scenic waterfalls and deep gorges to historic trails and coastal paths. Alabama promises unique adventures through its rich natural heritage and warm Southern hospitality.

Our upcoming Alabama hiking adventure will take us through forests, mountains, and along rivers that have shaped both the landscape and the culture of this fascinating state. We look forward to discovering hidden gems and creating new memories in Alabama's great outdoors.

Planned Hiking Destinations:

  • 🏔️ Cheaha State Park - Alabama's highest point with mountain views
  • 💧 DeSoto Falls - Stunning waterfall in northeastern Alabama
  • 🌲 Bankhead National Forest - Diverse ecosystems and rock formations
  • 🏖️ Gulf State Park - Coastal hiking and beach trails
  • 🌊 Little River Canyon - Deep gorge and scenic overlooks
