Our Pennsylvania Adventure

Pennsylvania offers us rich hiking heritage through the Appalachian Mountains, historic valleys, and pristine state forests that showcase both natural beauty and American history. The Keystone State provides diverse outdoor adventures from mountain peaks to peaceful woodlands.

Our Pennsylvania hiking adventure will take us along historic trails, through ancient forests, and past significant landmarks. We're excited to explore the natural and cultural treasures that make Pennsylvania a hiking destination with deep American roots.

Planned Must-See Destinations:

  • 🏔️ Appalachian Trail - Over 230 miles through Pennsylvania
  • 💧 Bushkill Falls - "Niagara of Pennsylvania"
  • 🌲 Allegheny National Forest - Northwestern Pennsylvania wilderness
  • 🏛️ Valley Forge - Historic trails and preserved battlefields
  • ⛰️ Pine Creek Gorge - "Pennsylvania Grand Canyon"
